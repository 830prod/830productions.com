Ask us directly
Do you shoot in RAW?

Yes. We shoot RAW whenever it gives us better colour and image quality in the edit.

Can I have the RAW files?

RAW files aren’t included in standard packages. For qualifying commercial projects, we offer a RAW file buyout.

How fast is delivery?

About 7 days for most projects, depending on size. Rush delivery can be added when the schedule allows.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. We travel throughout the tri-state area, and we can quote travel beyond it.

How do payments work?

Shop products are paid through Stripe checkout. Service projects are quoted directly and paid on the project terms.

Why can’t we book you on Saturdays?

830 Productions is closed from Friday night to Saturday night. No exceptions. Thanks for understanding.

Can you match our brand style?

Yes. Share your references and goals before the shoot, and we will match camera, lighting and the edit to them.
